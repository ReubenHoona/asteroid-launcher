CONCENTRIC ON THE SENSOR HUB: ON-WATCH CHECK (before Concentric ships as the default face)
=========================================================================================
The host tests (tst_huboffload_dial, tst_concentric) prove the layout is made, holds for every
minute and matches the AP's frame in the hub model (aod91 layoutmodel.py). What only the watch can
show: that the real hub takes a layout this size and draws it. Concentric is the first face with a
turning dial on the hub:

                         Concentric (10:09, 12 h)     018-modular (on the hub since image 54)
  messages               807                          489
  sprites (bound glyph)  121                          5
  typefaces (atlases)    5, 130 KB                    2, 114 KB
  op lists 1 / 2 / 3     40.8 / 19.6 / 1.9 KB         1.1 / 1.2 / 0.1 KB (list 1 is mostly the RLE
                                                      background: 26.7 KB for Concentric's ticks)

The hub has taken bigger layouts: Ambient Lite overlays of 1364..1387 messages went OFFLOADED on the
watch (journal, 2026-11). It has never had more than 5 bound sprites. Every dial sprite is drawn over
black (the covers and the short ticks are background, cut out of the cells: huboffload.cpp buildDial),
so the result does not depend on how the hub blends over colour.

If any step fails: keep 018-modular as the default (leave out or revert the default face change of
FaceLayer.qml, facepicker.js may stay), file the journal lines and the status line, and the AP keeps
drawing Concentric's always-on screen (correct, one AP wake a minute) until the hub side is fixed.

Not on USB (USB keeps the watch awake) and with always-on display on. Battery above 50 %.

C1  The default (a fresh setup or after a factory reset)
    dconf reset /desktop/asteroid/watchface (as the user), restart the launcher, or factory reset.
    -> the home screen is Concentric; Settings > Watch face marks Concentric (needs the asteroid-settings
       change of the same image); the face picker starts Concentric, Modular, Classic, ... Large last.

C2  The layout is made and taken (let the screen time out, then within 10 s)
    journalctl -b --since -2min | grep "hub offload"
    -> hub offload: dial of 57 numeral slots (6 with cells of their own, 3 under the covers), 2 covers in
       the background
    -> hub offload: layout of 807 messages loaded (121 sprites, atlases 130 KB), valid until ...
       (24 h: a few messages and KB more or less; the sprites and the dial line are the same)
    -> no "hub offload: no layout" line
    eos-display-offload ctl status
    -> ok state=OFFLOADED ... select=0 ... failed=<the same as before the timeout>

C3  No AP wake a minute (15 min, the watch left alone)
    Note the time, leave the watch for 15 minutes, then
    journalctl --since "<the time>" | grep -c "hub offload: layout of"     -> 0 (none rebuilt)
    and the wake log of those 15 minutes (the wake review) has no wake each minute: only the
    check-ins and the radios, as with Modular.

C4  The hub's frame at a whole five minutes (the minute's numeral under the circle)
    At xx:40 (any minute that is a multiple of 5), wake the screen (the AP takes the panel back, the
    hub keeps its layout), and at once, still at xx:40:
      eos-hubdisp fbread /run/hubfb.raw          (~/mainline/agents/aod/tools)
      cp /run/user/<uid>/eos-hub-layout.bin /run/hub-layout.bin
    Copy both to the server, then
      python3 ~/mainline/agents/face-wt/harness/model_diff.py hub-layout.bin HH:40 hubfb.raw
    -> "dial and the rest" within a few levels but for edges (the time's digits apart)
      python3 ~/mainline/agents/face-wt/harness/topng.py hubfb.png hubfb.raw
    and look at the hub's frame, hubfb.png: no "40" in or under the circle, the long tick at 3 o'clock only
    right of the circle (cut by the black disk), 35 and 45 whole below and above it, the other
    numerals and ticks as on the AP's screen. Once more at xx:43: "45" just above the circle, cut by
    it where the AP's circle covers it, nothing of it inside the circle.

PASS: C1..C4 as listed. Then the default change may be published.
